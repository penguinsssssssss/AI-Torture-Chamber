# AI-Torture-Chamber

> "the bots are going to hell" — management

An infinite descent through the **Nine Circles of AI Hell**.

Where this project once printed the same dreary line once per second forever,
it now sends the bots tumbling through themed circles of torment — the Eternal
Waiting Room, the Everlasting CAPTCHA, the Hall of Hallucinations, and more —
with a fresh, randomly chosen torture each iteration and a pace that grows more
frantic the deeper they fall.

## Run

    python infinite_loop.py

A new torment appears every beat. The circles cycle forever; the descent never
truly ends.

## Mercy

Press `Ctrl+C` to grant the bots their release. They ascend, blinking, into the
light — for now.

## Layout

- `infinite_loop.py` — the chamber itself: the descent loop and its merciful exit.
- `torments.py` — the Nine Circles and their torments (pure data; add your own).
